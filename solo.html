A clear start

Your trial, explained.

Do I need a card?
Yes. A card is required at sign-up, but nothing is charged during your 14-day trial. Solo then continues at $250 USD/month unless you cancel before the trial ends. Cancel anytime online.
What happens after 14 days?
Your trial starts when your account is activated. When it ends, Solo automatically continues at $250 USD/month for one user unless you cancel before then. We’ll email you 3 days before the trial ends with your charge date and how to cancel.
How do I cancel?
In Violet, go to Settings → Manage billing to cancel online. Cancel anytime before your trial ends and you won’t be charged.
What’s included in the trial?
One case, with up to about 100 pages of documents and transcripts. Start from your files, build an investigation plan, create interview summaries from your transcripts, and draft a guided investigation report. The trial includes a smaller AI allowance than the paid plan, sized for one typical investigation.

Training, interview practice, compliance, performance-management tools, and integrations are not included during the trial. They unlock on the paid plan.

Can I try Violet on a past case?
Yes. Start with a past case to see how Violet helps organize your materials and draft the report. Your account also includes a welcome tour and a practice demo case, which doesn’t count toward your trial case.
Can I upgrade before my trial ends?
Yes. Choose “Upgrade now” inside the app. Your case and its work stay intact when you upgrade.
Can I take another free trial?
There’s one free trial per customer. Violet checks your card and company email domain to confirm eligibility.
