Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 15, 2026

Violet Investigations (“Violet,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a California-based company that provides an AI-powered workplace investigation case management platform. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our platform at app.violetinvestigations.com (the “Service”). This policy is designed to comply with applicable privacy laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), the Canadian Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), and British Columbia’s Personal Information Protection Act (BC PIPA).

Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your name, email address, and organization details through our authentication provider, Clerk.
  • Investigation Data: Case details, interview notes, exhibits, and other materials you upload or create within the platform.
  • Tell Violet Reports: Information provided by reporters through our web-based or phone-based reporting channels, including conversation transcripts and call recordings.
  • Voice Data: When reporters use Tell Violet’s voice features (web voice chat or phone hotline), we collect audio recordings and AI-generated transcripts. See “Biometric Data and Voice Recordings” below for details.
  • Usage Data: We collect standard log data such as IP addresses, browser type, and pages visited to maintain and improve the Service.

AI Processing and Automated Decision-Making

Violet uses artificial intelligence, powered by Anthropic’s Claude, to generate investigation artifacts such as investigation plans, interview outlines, credibility analyses, policy drafts, gap analyses, and investigation reports. Your investigation data is sent to Anthropic’s API for processing. Anthropic does not use your data to train its models.

All AI-generated content is provided as a draft only and does not constitute legal advice. The assigned investigator or qualified counsel is responsible for reviewing, editing, and approving all AI-generated content before it is finalized or shared. Violet is an AI-assisted drafting tool — it does not replace professional judgment or legal counsel.

Automated Decision-Making Technology (ADMT): Violet’s AI features assist with drafting investigation artifacts and analyzing case data. These features are designed to support human decision-making, not replace it. No employment decisions (including discipline, termination, or hiring) are made by Violet’s AI. All AI outputs require human review and approval before finalization. You have the right to opt out of automated decision-making technology. To exercise this right, contact us at privacy@violetinvestigations.com.

Subprocessors

We use the following third-party services to operate the platform:

  • Anthropic — AI processing (Claude API). Processes investigation data to generate AI artifacts. Does not retain or train on customer data.
  • Clerk — Authentication and user management
  • Railway — Application hosting and database
  • Vercel — Frontend hosting
  • Cloudflare — File storage (R2), DNS, CDN, and email routing
  • Google Cloud — Cloud services and document export (Google Docs)
  • Deepgram — Audio transcription of interview recordings
  • ElevenLabs — Audio narration for compliance training modules (no customer case data)
  • Retell AI — Voice AI for Tell Violet reporting hotline and Practice with Violet training exercises
  • Resend — Transactional email delivery
  • Stripe — Payment processing

This list is current as of March 30, 2026.

Tell Violet (Reporting Channels)

Violet provides web-based and phone-based reporting channels for employees to submit workplace concerns.

Web Chat and Web Voice: Reporters interact with an AI system, not a human. For web voice conversations, reporters must provide explicit consent via an on-screen prompt before the conversation begins. The conversation is recorded and transcribed by AI.

Phone Hotline: Reporters are informed at the start of every call that: (1) the call is recorded, (2) the call will be transcribed and analyzed by AI, (3) the reporter is speaking with an AI assistant, not a human, and (4) information will be shared with the investigations team on a need-to-know basis. Verbal consent is required before the call proceeds. If a caller declines consent, the call is ended immediately and no data is collected.

Anonymous Reporting: Reporters may choose to remain anonymous. Anonymous reports do not include the reporter’s name. Reporters who provide an email address may receive follow-up communications through an anonymized email relay that protects their identity from the investigations team.

Biometric Data and Voice Recordings

Violet’s phone hotline and web voice features collect audio recordings for transcription and analysis. We do not knowingly create, store, or use voiceprints or biometric voice identifiers for speaker identification or verification purposes.

If you are an Illinois resident, or a resident of any state with biometric privacy protections, and you believe biometric data may have been collected in connection with your use of the Service, you may contact us at privacy@violetinvestigations.com to request disclosure, a copy, or deletion of any such data.

AI Data Processing and Model Training

Violet uses artificial intelligence to assist with investigation workflows, including drafting investigation plans, interview outlines, and reports. To provide these features, certain case data is processed through third-party AI services.

We do not train AI models on your data. Customer case data, uploaded documents, investigation reports, and AI-generated outputs are never used to train, fine-tune, or improve any AI model — whether operated by Violet or by our third-party AI providers. Your data is processed solely to generate the specific output you requested and is not retained by our AI providers for model improvement purposes.

Our primary AI provider, Anthropic, processes data through their commercial API under terms that prohibit the use of API inputs and outputs for model training. For more information, see Anthropic’s API data usage policy.

Confidentiality of Customer Data

All data submitted to Violet — including case details, party information, uploaded documents, interview transcripts, AI-generated artifacts, and investigation reports — is treated as confidential.

Violet does not share, sell, or disclose customer case data to any third party except as strictly necessary to provide the Service through the following subprocessors:

  • Anthropic — AI processing for investigation artifacts. Data is processed via Anthropic’s commercial API and is not used for model training.
  • Cloudflare — File storage (R2) and content delivery.
  • Railway — Application and database hosting.
  • Clerk — User authentication and identity management.
  • Deepgram — Audio transcription of interview recordings.
  • ElevenLabs — Audio narration for compliance training modules.
  • Retell AI — Voice AI for the Tell Violet reporting hotline and Practice with Violet training exercises.
  • Resend — Transactional email delivery.

Each subprocessor is contractually obligated to maintain confidentiality and use customer data solely to provide their respective services. We will update this list if we add or change subprocessors that handle customer case data.

Data Ownership

Your organization owns all data you create or upload to the platform, including all AI-generated content such as investigation plans, interview outlines, and investigation reports. Violet does not claim ownership of any customer data or AI-generated work product. We use your data solely to provide the Service. We do not use customer case data to train AI models or for any purpose unrelated to delivering the features you use.

Data Retention

We retain your data as follows:

  • Active accounts: Data is retained for the duration of your active subscription.
  • Cancelled accounts: Upon cancellation, data is retained for 30 days to allow for reactivation or export. After 30 days, all customer data is permanently deleted from our systems and backups.
  • Tell Violet reports: Report data, including transcripts and recordings, is retained for the duration of the associated organization’s active subscription and subject to the same cancellation policy above.
  • Voice recordings: Audio recordings from phone and web voice interactions are retained for the duration of the associated organization’s subscription. Organizations may request earlier deletion by contacting us.
  • Legal holds: If we are notified that data is subject to a legal hold or preservation obligation, we will retain the relevant data for the duration of that obligation regardless of the account’s subscription status.

We recommend that customers establish their own data retention policies in accordance with applicable federal and state record-keeping requirements, including EEOC (1 year from termination), ADEA (3 years), FLSA (3 years), and any applicable state requirements.

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Multi-tenant isolation ensuring organizations can only access their own data
  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • Role-based access controls within organizations
  • Regular security reviews of our infrastructure and codebase

California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act:

  • Right to Know: You may request that we disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the sources from which we collected it, our business purpose for collecting it, and the categories of third parties with whom we share it.
  • Right to Delete: You may request that we delete the personal information we have collected about you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to Correct: You may request that we correct inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.
  • Right to Opt Out of Sale or Sharing: Violet does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information: To the extent we process sensitive personal information (such as investigation data containing allegations or witness statements), we use it only to provide the Service.
  • Right to Opt Out of Automated Decision-Making: You may opt out of the use of automated decision-making technology. See “AI Processing and Automated Decision-Making” above.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@violetinvestigations.com. We will respond to verifiable requests within 45 days.

Canadian Privacy Rights (PIPEDA and BC PIPA)

If you are located in Canada, your personal information is protected under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, if you are in British Columbia, the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). In addition to the rights described elsewhere in this policy, Canadian users have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and receive an account of its use and disclosure
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information
  • Withdraw consent for future collection, use, or disclosure of your personal information, subject to legal or contractual restrictions
  • File a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in British Columbia, the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner

We will respond to access and correction requests within 30 days.

Cross-Border Data Transfers: Violet’s infrastructure is hosted in the United States. By using our services, your personal information may be transferred to and processed in the United States. We ensure that your information receives a comparable level of protection through contractual safeguards, encryption in transit and at rest, strict access controls, and our commitment not to use your data for AI model training. If you have questions about our cross-border data practices, contact privacy@violetinvestigations.com.

Data Retention and Deletion: We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected or as required by law. You may request deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting privacy@violetinvestigations.com. Upon receiving a verified deletion request, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or necessary for the completion of an active investigation matter.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on the Service and updating the effective date. Your continued use of the Service after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at privacy@violetinvestigations.com.
