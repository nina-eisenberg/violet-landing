Violet provides web-based and phone-based reporting channels (“Tell Violet”) for employees to submit workplace concerns. When using Tell Violet:

  • Reporters are informed that they are interacting with an AI system, not a human.
  • Phone calls are recorded. At the start of every call, reporters are informed that the call is recorded and will be transcribed and analyzed by AI. Verbal consent is required before the call proceeds. Callers who decline consent are not recorded and the call is ended.
