Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a card?
Yes. A card is required at sign-up, but nothing is charged during your 14-day trial. Solo then continues at $250 USD/month unless you cancel before the trial ends. Cancel anytime online.
What happens after 14 days?
Your trial starts when your account is activated. When it ends, Solo automatically continues at $250 USD/month for one user unless you cancel before then. We’ll email you 3 days before the trial ends with your charge date and how to cancel.
How do I cancel?
In Violet, go to Settings → Manage billing to cancel online. Cancel anytime before your trial ends and you won’t be charged.
What’s included in the trial?
One case, with up to about 100 pages of documents and transcripts. Start from your files, build an investigation plan, create interview summaries from your transcripts, and draft a guided investigation report. The trial includes a smaller AI allowance than the paid plan, sized for one typical investigation.

Training, interview practice, compliance, performance-management tools, and integrations are not included during the trial. They unlock on the paid plan.

Is Solo built for independent investigators?
Yes. Solo gives one external workplace investigator a private workspace for managing matters, preparing interviews, analyzing the record, and drafting reports. Employer and multi-investigator plans remain available separately.
How does the monthly AI Usage Allowance work?

Each paid investigator receives a separate monthly AI Usage Allowance. The allowance is designed to support approximately eight reports involving investigations of typical size and complexity, but the number of reports will vary based on source volume, report complexity, drafting, regeneration, and verification activity.

AI usage begins to accrue when AI-assisted processing starts. In Violet’s guided-report workflow, that includes beginning AI-assisted drafting; a report does not need to be finalized or filed before the associated activity uses the allowance.

If an investigator exhausts the allowance, Violet may pause the initiation of new AI-assisted processing until the next monthly billing period. Existing cases, content, and reports remain available for access, manual editing, download, and export. Additional capacity requires agreement to a custom plan, and Violet does not impose automatic overage charges.

How large an investigation can Violet handle?

Violet can technically support investigations with roughly 40 one-hour interviews, 20 interviews plus about 500 pages of exhibits, or 7 full-day interviews. Capacity depends on the combined length of transcripts and documents, so the mix matters more than the number of interviews.

The Solo trial is limited to one case with up to about 100 pages of documents and transcripts. These larger estimates describe Violet’s technical processing capacity, not the amount of AI processing included in every subscription. A matter approaching these limits may consume a substantial portion of an investigator’s monthly AI Usage Allowance or require a custom plan.

Does Violet make legal decisions?
No. Violet drafts, you decide. The investigator makes each determination and approves the final report. Violet checks the determination against the evidence, drafts the analysis, and flags material claims or citations for review. All AI outputs include appropriate disclaimers and should be reviewed by qualified professionals.
What reports are available on paid plans?
Investigation reports with credibility analysis, interview outlines, investigation plans, triage memos, written warnings, employee handbooks, and quarterly executive reports with industry benchmarking, all generated from your data with one click.
Where is my data stored?
Your data is hosted in the United States on production-grade infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit. Investigation data is never used to train AI models. Each organization's data is fully isolated with no cross-tenant access.
Can I export my data?
Yes. Settings → “Export my data” produces a complete, downloadable copy of your organization’s cases, investigation reports, hotline submissions, case documents and files, and structured data — organized as readable per-case folders of PDFs alongside the underlying data. Investigation reports also export to Google Docs, quarterly reports to PDF and PowerPoint, and handbooks to PDF, Word, or web.
What access controls are available?
Violet includes role-based access control so you can control who sees what. Assign roles from Admin down to Viewer, and every action is scoped to your organization. Investigators only see their own cases, managers see their team's, and admins control it all.
What compliance standards does Violet follow?
Violet is built with security-first architecture: encrypted data, tenant isolation, input validation, and rate-limited APIs. SOC 2 readiness, bias testing, and vendor security documentation are on our 2026 roadmap. We're happy to complete your security questionnaire.
Can I cancel and take my data with me?
Yes. You can manage or cancel your subscription through billing. If your subscription ends, your workspace enters a recovery period and you can create a downloadable export of your cases, reports, transcripts, documents, and audit log.
