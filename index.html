Full Tell Violet Transcript

March 21, 2026 · Fictional sample conversation

Violet

Can you tell me what happened?

Alex

After I raised a concern, I stopped being invited to the team meetings.

Violet

When did you first notice the invitations had stopped?

Alex

March 10. I saw the meeting on a colleague’s calendar, but it wasn’t on mine. I had been attending the weekly project meetings before that.

Violet

Tell me about the concern you raised before that happened.

Alex

I spoke with my manager, Jordan Lee, on March 3 about a workplace concern. After that conversation, the invitations stopped.

Violet

What makes you connect the change in invitations with that conversation?

Alex

The timing. I don’t know who actually changed the invitation list or what they were thinking. But it happened the following week, and nobody explained it to me.

Violet

Were you given any explanation for the change?

Alex

Not that I recall. I don’t know whether the meeting group changed for other people too.

Violet

How has missing those meetings affected your work?

Alex

That’s where the team discusses project decisions. Without the invitations, I’m worried I’m missing information I need to do my job.

Violet

What records or people might help someone understand what happened?

Alex

The old and new calendar invitations should show the change. Jordan would know about the meeting group. I can share the invitations I have.

Violet

Is there anything else you would like the person reviewing this report to understand?

Alex

I want to understand why I was removed and make sure I can raise a concern without losing access to my team’s work.

Violet

Thank you for sharing this. Your report will be available for review. Is there anything you want to correct or add?

Alex

No, that captures what I wanted to report. My name is Alex Morgan.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who is Violet built for?
HR leaders, compliance teams, employee relations professionals, and workplace investigators. Whether you need to train 50 employees on California's SB 1343, investigate a harassment complaint, or generate a quarterly board report — Violet handles the full compliance lifecycle.
Does Violet make legal decisions?
No. Violet drafts, you decide. The investigator sets the findings and recommendations. Violet writes the supporting analysis. A built-in confidence check warns you if the evidence doesn't support your conclusion. All AI outputs include appropriate disclaimers and should be reviewed by qualified professionals.
What reports come built in?
Investigation reports with credibility analysis, interview outlines, investigation plans, triage memos, written warnings, employee handbooks, and quarterly executive reports with industry benchmarking, all generated from your data with one click.
Where is my data stored?
Your data is hosted in the United States on production-grade infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit. Investigation data is never used to train AI models. Each organization's data is fully isolated with no cross-tenant access.
Can I export my data?
Yes. Settings → “Export my data” produces a complete, downloadable copy of your organization’s cases, investigation reports, hotline submissions, case documents and files, and structured data — organized as readable per-case folders of PDFs alongside the underlying data. Investigation reports also export to Google Docs, quarterly reports to PDF and PowerPoint, and handbooks to PDF, Word, or web.
What access controls are available?
Violet includes role-based access control so you can control who sees what. Assign roles from Admin down to Viewer, and every action is scoped to your organization. Investigators only see their own cases, managers see their team's, and admins control it all.
What compliance standards does Violet follow?
Violet is built with security-first architecture: encrypted data, tenant isolation, input validation, and rate-limited APIs. SOC 2 readiness, bias testing, and vendor security documentation are on our 2026 roadmap. We're happy to complete your security questionnaire.
Can external investigators use Violet?
Absolutely. The Violet Investigator plan is designed specifically for solo and external investigators. You get AI investigation reports, AI interview outlines, Google Drive integration, and Coach Violet without needing the intake or policy features that internal teams use.
