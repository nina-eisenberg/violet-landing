Leah Goldberg, Member Services Representative II in the Contact Center, filed a formal complaint with Priya Anand in HR on September 15, 2026, against her supervisor, Marcus Doyle, Supervisor, Contact Center. Goldberg states that on September 4, 2026, she emailed Doyle asking not to be scheduled on Friday evenings past 5 PM because she observes Shabbat, and that a new Q4 rotation beginning September 18, 2026 would place all employees on a Friday 2–10 PM shift. She reports that Doyle did not discuss the request with her, did not engage with a shift swap that her coworker Jordan Kim offered, and denied the request about ten days later, which she says exceeds the five days the policy allows. She also alleges that at a team huddle on September 9, 2026, Doyle said "Fine" while looking at her and stated "We'll see about Fridays," and that Kim later told her Doyle had commented about people wanting their "holy day" off. Goldberg reports feeling that her religion is not respected on the team.

Harlow & Pine Credit Union – Religious Accommodation Policy (excerpt) HR-114, effective January 1, 2026 1. Harlow & Pine accommodates employees' sincerely held religious beliefs and practices unless the accommodation would cause undue hardship. 2. A supervisor who receives a request must respond in writing within five (5) business days. Business days exclude weekends and company holidays. 3. Before denying a request, the supervisor must consider reasonable alternatives, including voluntary shift swaps, schedule changes and transfers, and must discuss them with the employee. 4. A supervisor who believes a request cannot be granted must consult HR before denying it. 5. Company holidays in 2026 include Labor Day, Monday, September 7. 6. No employee may be retaliated against for requesting an accommodation or for raising a concern under this policy.
