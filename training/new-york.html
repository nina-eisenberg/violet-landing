New York Requirements at a Glance

👥
Who Must Comply All employers in New York State, regardless of size (NYSHRL applies to all)
⏱
Training Track Single track for all employees — no separate supervisor requirement under state law
📅
Frequency Annual training required for all employees
✅
Format Required Interactive: must include examples, be provided in the employee's primary language

Applicable Statutes

  • NY Labor Law § 201-g State-level training mandate for all employers, annual interactive training
  • NYC Local Law 96 (2018) NYC-specific requirements for employers with 15+ employees in New York City
  • NYCHRL New York City Human Rights Law — broader protections and definitions than state law
