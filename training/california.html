California Requirements at a Glance

👥
Who Must Comply Employers with 5 or more employees (including temporary and seasonal workers)
⏱
Training Duration 1 hour for nonsupervisory employees; 2 hours for supervisors and managers
📅
Frequency Every 2 years, within 6 months of hire for new employees
✅
Format Required Interactive: must include questions, skill-building activities, and hypothetical scenarios
Course Modules

Nonsupervisory Track (1 Hour)

Required for all nonsupervisory employees. Covers definitions, examples, reporting, and bystander intervention.

  • 1
    What Is Harassment? Legal definitions under FEHA, types of harassment, and protected characteristics
  • 2
    Sexual Harassment Defined Quid pro quo vs. hostile work environment, verbal, physical, and visual examples
  • 3
    Abusive Conduct & Bullying California's abusive conduct requirement (AB 2053) and how it differs from harassment
  • 4
    Discrimination & Retaliation Connection between harassment and discrimination, anti-retaliation protections
  • 5
    Real-World Scenarios Branching scenarios with choices and consequences based on California case law
  • 6
    Bystander Intervention Strategies for safely intervening when witnessing harassment (SB 1343 requirement)
  • 7
    Reporting & Resources Internal complaint process, DFEH/CRD filing, and available remedies
  • 8
    Practice Conversation with Violet Live AI conversation where you practice responding to a harassment concern

Supervisor-Additional Modules (+1 Hour)

Additional modules required for supervisors and managers, covering their specific legal obligations.

  • 9
    Supervisor Liability Personal liability, employer strict liability, and the supervisor's duty to act
  • 10
    Handling Complaints Steps for receiving, documenting, and escalating harassment complaints
  • 11
    Investigation Obligations Prompt, thorough, and impartial investigations required by California law
  • 12
    Corrective Action & Prevention Remediation, monitoring, and building a harassment-free workplace culture
  • 13
    Supervisor Practice Scenario Advanced branching scenario where you manage a harassment complaint as a supervisor
